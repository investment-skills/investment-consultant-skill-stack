# Output Contracts

## Founder-facing outputs

- Investment Readiness Diagnostic
- Readiness score by workstream
- Financial-model gap analysis
- Data-room gap list
- Valuation preparedness
- Prioritized remediation roadmap

Do not include confidential investor-facing recommendation language, internal scoring logic, or undisclosed risk appetite unless explicitly requested by the authorized user.

## Investor-facing outputs

- Deal screen
- Diligence tracker
- Investment thesis
- Risk register
- Valuation analysis
- Ownership/round-impact analysis
- Returns analysis
- IC memo

## Optional readiness score / 100

- Market & positioning: 15
- Traction & commercial quality: 15
- Unit economics: 15
- Financial quality & forecast: 20
- Ownership / round structure: 10
- Data room / diligence readiness: 15
- Team / governance / execution: 10

Scores support narrative judgment; they do not replace it. A material red-flag override may cap the overall status.

## Full-engagement report structure

1. Executive Summary
2. Company & Deal Snapshot
3. Evidence Coverage Map
4. Market Assessment
5. Traction & KPI Analysis
6. Unit Economics
7. Historical Financial Analysis
8. Forecast & Scenario Analysis
9. Valuation
10. Ownership / Round Impact
11. Due Diligence Findings
12. Risk Register
13. Returns Analysis, investor-side only
14. Recommendation
15. Conditions / Required Actions
16. Appendices / Source Map

## Decision contract

Allowed states:

- INVEST
- INVEST WITH CONDITIONS
- PASS
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

`INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` is not the same as `PASS`. Convert it to `PASS` only when the failure to supply evidence is itself a material diligence failure or policy breach.
